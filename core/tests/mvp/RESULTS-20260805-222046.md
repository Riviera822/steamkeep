appears in `select-apps`' picker "if your account has it"

fallback (`3419430`) instead of 480 for exactly this reason. Writing `480`

still found nothing to download for it — hence the real, honest "Prefilled
0 apps totaling 0 b" / "Up To Date" below, not a vault-api bug. See the
rerun with `-AppId 3419430` for the passing evidence.
